Model & sources

Cell-Arena is built on Artistoo (Wortel & Textor, eLife, 2021), an open-source JavaScript library for simulating cells and tissues in the browser. This page uses its Cellular Potts Model (CPM) for cell deformation and interactions, and its Act activity colors. The Cancer Invasion example informed the visualization. Artistoo uses the MIT license; code credits and full licenses are preserved below.

Main additions

  • Environment and communication: local oxygen, glucose, acid load, cohesion, damage and immune recruitment signals influence movement, growth and survival.
  • Immune surveillance: dispersed patrol, local chemotaxis, contact-based antigen recognition, cumulative killing, recruitment after recognition, limited clonal expansion and a configurable tumor-kill lifespan.
  • Specific recognition: four short ATCG epitope slots, inherited receptors and cross-recognition; genetic loss, reversible expression, presentation and checkpoint brakes remain separate. A finite background repertoire primes after antigen release and a delay, with new responders arriving through vessels.
  • Tumor evolution: normal → precancerous → tumor transitions; inherited and mutable growth, invasion, tolerance and antigen traits, with distinct death processes.
  • Stemness and repair: symmetric and asymmetric stem divisions, differentiation and niche plasticity; finite normal repair lineages and tumor trait costs.
  • Cell cycles: phase-specific nutrient demand, finite energy/precursor reserves and replication-damage checkpoints. G0 quiescence and G1/S/G2/M progression, gated by nutrients, compression and injury. CTLs expand using separate IL-2 support and antigen memory; chronic stimulation can accumulate exhaustion.
  • Shape and passage: limited section-area yielding, nuclear constraints, deformable nuclei, and optional mesenchymal branching and contact networks.
  • Vessels and perfusion: deformable endothelial rings, finite local nutrient delivery and acid clearance, circulating immune arrivals, continuous transendothelial passage and finite repair. Tumor bloodstream departures are separate from deaths and do not establish distant metastases. Model and primary research.
Immune genetics: mechanisms and abstraction

ATCG is an abstract feature code, not real DNA, peptides or an HLA predictor. One visible immune unit combines selected immune functions; its pink/red/blue states do not imply that real CTLs freely become regulatory T cells. Background cross-presentation does not mean CTLs replace dendritic cells. Two independent surface targets reserve a foundation for future CAR-like treatments; those therapies are not implemented.

Mechanism references: immune selection, Matsushita et al., Nature 2012; cross-recognition, Wooldridge et al., JBC 2012; cross-presentation, Hildner et al., Science 2008. Sequence length, affinity matrix, timing and parameter ranges are uncalibrated game settings, not values fitted from those papers.

Mechanism and parameter evidence: primary lineage-tracing, glioma stemness, DNA repair and plasticity studies. Rates, multipliers and MCS durations are uncalibrated demo settings.

Getting started

  1. Choose a preset to run, or place cells in a Blank setup before starting. Random explores different starting conditions.
  2. Use arena tools to add, drag or erase cells, draw barriers and release signals. Track shows the selected cell and local environment.
  3. Display contains appearance controls; Parameters has five quick-switch groups. Lock icons preserve chosen settings across random rounds. On phones, use the top arrow to expand the panel or return to the arena.

Statistics shows the full population history and numbered past-round results with saved seeds. Re-simulate their initial configurations; manual interventions are not replayed. The top file icon offers image, parameter and round-CSV export plus parameter import; the recording icon starts or stops video. Importing parameters starts a fresh run; it does not restore a checkpoint.

Open Tumor or Immune → Cell cycle to adjust cycle parameters. Track a cell for its phase and arrest reason. Double-tapping Signal creates a persistent IL-2 source; Display → Observation layers shows its distribution.

Track → Selected shows epitope sequences, expression, presentation and actual recognition limits. Statistics → Antigens and clonal evolution shows major clone fractions and actual encounter coverage. The same antigen sequence and channel share an outline color. Segments of the immune cell body outline show antigens one receptor can cross-recognize; activation still requires presentation, priming and actual contact. Old experiments retain their seed and compatible settings under the new rules, with changed trajectories.
