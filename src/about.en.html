Model & sources

Cell-Arena is built on Artistoo (Wortel & Textor, eLife, 2021), an open-source JavaScript library for simulating cells and tissues in the browser. This page uses its Cellular Potts Model (CPM) for cell deformation and interactions, and its Act activity colors. The Cancer Invasion example informed the visualization. Artistoo uses the MIT license; code credits and full licenses are preserved below.

Main additions

  • Environment and communication: local oxygen, glucose, acid load, cohesion, damage and immune recruitment signals influence movement, growth and survival.
  • Immune surveillance: dispersed patrol, local chemotaxis, contact-based antigen recognition, cumulative killing, recruitment after recognition and limited clonal expansion.
  • Tumor evolution: normal → abnormal → tumor transitions; inherited and mutable growth, invasion, tolerance and antigen traits, with distinct death processes.
  • Shape and passage: limited section-area yielding, nuclear constraints, deformable nuclei, and optional mesenchymal branching and contact networks.

Getting started

  1. Choose a preset to run, or place cells in a Blank setup before starting. Randomize explores different starting conditions.
  2. Use arena tools to add, drag or erase cells, draw barriers and release signals. Inspect cell shows the selected cell and local environment.
  3. Adjust cells and their environment in six parameter groups. On phones, tap Parameters to expand the editor, type a value or use + / −, then tap Back to arena.

Statistics shows the full population history. At the bottom of the panel, export or import parameters, save an image or record a video. Importing parameters starts a fresh run; it does not restore a checkpoint.
